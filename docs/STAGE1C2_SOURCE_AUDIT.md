# Stage 1C.2 Stage 1 — source evidence and limits

This is a data-quality investigation, not approval for allocation fitting. The exact public-file URLs, byte hashes, GitHub asset timestamps and actual retrieval times are in the committed bundle's `source_index.json`. Only bounded regular-season rows are materialised; 2026 week 11 onward and all later seasons remain excluded.

Sources inspected on 2026-11-14:

- [nflreadr weekly roster loader](https://github.com/nflverse/nflreadr/blob/main/R/load_rosters_weekly.R): weekly versions are season files served from the `weekly_rosters` nflverse-data release. These have season/week/team/player/status fields but no original row-level publication/receipt history. Week labels are not timestamps. The inspected 2025 asset's creation date is 2026-03-14.
- [Roster field dictionary](https://github.com/nflverse/nflreadr/blob/main/data-raw/dictionary_rosters.csv): status covers active, inactive, reserve and practice-squad conditions. Transaction codes are not interchangeable with final game-day active lists. Do not treat every non-ACT record as inactive. Position/depth-chart labels are not verified predecision role evidence.
- [nflreadr participation loader](https://github.com/nflverse/nflreadr/blob/main/R/load_participation.R): explicitly states, "Participation data from 2023 onwards is courtesy of FTN and is provided after all post-season games are completed." This release is excluded as an in-season share feature. No FTN participation payload was downloaded or incorporated into this study.
- [nflreadr injury loader](https://github.com/nflverse/nflreadr/blob/main/R/load_injuries.R): describes weekly API injury reports, not complete inactive lists. The actual inspected Parquet schema has `date_modified` in 2019–24 and lacks it in 2025–26. Missing dates remain missing. A retrospective publisher date is not an independently archived receipt or immutable historical version.
- [nflverse weekly rosters](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases/tag/weekly_rosters), [snap counts](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases/tag/snap_counts), [injuries](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases/tag/injuries): current revised artifacts are preserved in an external raw cache, with only minimal bounded fields committed. Snap evidence establishes retrospective participation; it does not prove when a roster or inactive designation became available.
- [nflverse player IDs](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases/tag/players): only permanent PFR/GSIS ID pairs are read. Current team, position, status, biography and future career fields are not loaded. The crosswalk is later-discovered identity evidence, never an as-of roster. Disagreement with a same-week identity link remains unresolved.
- Original PBP: exact cached hashes from the frozen Stage 1C input manifest are required. The revised descriptions, structured credits and original recorded box/team counters often share upstream data. Accepted repairs establish a documented, internally reconciled event ledger, not independent official certification. No benchmark label is replaced.

Independent public ESPN scoreboard/box-record requests to `site.api.espn.com` failed at the environment's network proxy. That exact domain has been requested for access; the audited bundle makes no independent corroboration claim. This is a retrieval limitation, not a claim that the records do not exist. Authoritative gamebooks/final credit records remain desirable for the five unresolved lateral/fumble/penalty cases.

The full raw payloads and downloaded documentation are retained under `/tmp/stage1c2-evidence`; original PBP remains under `/tmp/stage1c-pbp`. Git contains small bounded snapshots sufficient for exact data-decision replay. Re-fetching a mutable URL does not reproduce its recorded byte version automatically. Development prototypes are preserved separately under `/workspace/stage1c2-data-quality-prototype-v*`; they are not eligible audit bundles or benchmarks.

No licensed or paid provider was configured. No post-season-only participation chart, injury-exit outcome or actual-participant roster is used as a feasible predecision feature. No future outcome period is opened to supplement missing historical evidence.
